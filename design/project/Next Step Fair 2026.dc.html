About the event

The weeks that decide the next four years

Next Step began in 2023 with 9 universities in one hall in Sulaimani. The 2025 edition drew 14,600 visitors and 28 institutions. In 2026 the fair runs for three days and adds a policy conference on its opening day.

The organisation works with the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, and is a registered SDG Acceleration Action with UN DESA.

Everything at the fair is free for students, including seminars, workshops, Zankoline support desks and one-to-one advice.
